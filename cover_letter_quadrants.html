I. Genesis · Sensory Data 01

01. Career & Journey

Most applications start with a list of buzzwords. Let me begin with a tangible sensation: the rhythmic low frequency hum of MareNostrum 5 calculating climate digital twins across thousands of GPUs, or the microtonal chime of an embedded motion sphere when my cat Pipa bats it across the room.

Data is not merely an abstract metric confined to spreadsheet rows and logs. Following pioneers like Domestic Data Streamers, I treat data as raw, physical matter—something to be felt, heard, and spatially inhabited.

Scientific computing is uncovering the deepest mechanics of our climate and energy systems. But when breakthroughs remain locked inside cold cluster nodes, we have only finished half the mission. My obsession is the democratization of science: transforming petascale simulations into unforgettable, visceral human experiences.
