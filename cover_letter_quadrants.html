I. Genesis · Sensory Data

A Sound Instead of a Buzzword

Most letters start with a catalogue of adjectives. Let me begin with a sound: the low hum of MareNostrum 5 calculating climate turbulence across thousands of GPUs, or the microtonal chime of an embedded motion sphere when my cat Pipa bats it across the living room.

Data is not just an analytical output to be confined to rows and columns. Following pioneers like Domestic Data Streamers, I believe data is a living physical material—something to be felt, heard, and inhabited.

Science is uncovering the mechanics of our universe, but when those breakthroughs remain locked behind cold cluster logs, we have only completed half the mission. My obsession is the democratization of science: turning invisible computational realities into unforgettable, sensorial human stories.

03
III. Studio · Physical Computing

Four Personal Creative Experiments

MNusic — Listening to & Visualizing MN5 & LUMI Translating GPU load, memory bandwidth, and thermal gradients into real-time harmonic soundscapes (Web Audio API) and generative particles. Making Europe’s supercomputers audible.
Pipa-Music-Ball — Bio-Kinetic Interactive Artifact Microcontroller sphere (ESP32, 6-DoF IMU) mapping feline kinetic vectors into microtonal scales. Proof that physical data can be playful, tactile, and non-anthropocentric.
Expo-Curator Studio (github.com/carrommad/expo-curator) Open application for automated image classification, booklet publishing, and spatial exhibition wall staging. Engineered for museum curators and science disseminators.
Lens & Motion — Photography & After Effects Architectural still framing paired with procedural motion graphics and kinetic typography in After Effects, elevating raw data plots into cinematic visual narratives.
