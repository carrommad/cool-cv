# Assets Directory Structure

Welcome to your media workspace! You can drop your personal media and project outputs here. The web application (`index.html`) is configured to look in these folders.

## Folder Organization

### 1. `assets/videos/after-effects/`
- **Drop here:** Motion design experiments, procedural graphics, audio-reactive renders, or kinetic physics clips made with Adobe After Effects (e.g. `.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`).
- **Suggested filenames:**
  - `ae_motion_sample_1.mp4`
  - `ae_generative_telemetry.mp4`
  - `ae_particle_flow.mp4`

### 2. `assets/curator-selection/`
- **Drop here:** Curation outputs, booklet designs, sub-classification contact sheets, and exhibition wall mockups exported from [`expo-curator`](https://github.com/carrommad/expo-curator) (e.g. `.png`, `.jpg`, `.pdf`).
- **Suggested filenames:**
  - `wall_layout_01.png`
  - `booklet_spread.png`
  - `curated_grid.jpg`

### 3. `assets/photos/`
- **Drop here:** Your photography portfolio shots, street or documentary photography, architectural textures, or camera work.
- **Suggested filenames:**
  - `photo_01.jpg`
  - `photo_02.jpg`

### 4. `assets/docs/`
- **Drop here:** Reference PDFs, certificates, publications, or thesis summaries.

---
*Note: Any image or video placed in these folders can be embedded or rendered dynamically into the portfolio wall or linked in the CV.*
