Vision & Philosophy: Computational Rigor Meets Creative Expression

Hi there! ✨

I am a Computational Physicist (Double Degree in Physics & Mathematics, MSc in Plasma Physics) and HPC Scientific Software Engineer at the Barcelona Supercomputing Center (BSC). My career has bridged mathematical formalism with real-world, high-stakes computational problems across supercomputing, energy, and aerospace engineering.

Across diverse roles and European institutions, I have developed a unified methodology for tackling complex physical systems:

  • Non-Convex Multi-Dimensional Optimization (Cognitive Radar · Fraunhofer FHR) — Navigating non-convex, multimodal parameter spaces using heuristic search and simulated annealing. Designed non-planar radar antenna geometry in MATLAB to optimize radiation patterns and beam directivity under tight geometric constraints.
  • Industrial Data Science & Sensor Anomaly Detection (Renewable Energy · CENER) — Building end-to-end Python pipelines to analyze high-frequency SCADA time-series telemetry from offshore wind farms. Engineered predictive classification models with Scikit-Learn and Pandas to anticipate mechanical degradation and optimize turbine maintenance.
  • Particle Tracking & Symplectic Integration (HPC Plasma Physics · Seville & BSC) — Developing high-order symplectic particle integrators to trace charged particle trajectories and loss cones through turbulent magnetic fields. Translating kinetic and magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) equations into scalable parallel diagnostics.

Treating agentic workflows and LLMs as fluid thinking partners enables seamless transitions between C++/Fortran HPC kernels, microcontroller firmware, and interactive web tools.

Whether profiling parallel efficiency on Tier-0 supercomputers (MN5, LUMI), modeling complex physical dynamics, or designing interactive tools, my goal remains constant: pairing mathematical rigor with clear, compelling delivery.

I am always keen to collaborate with teams pushing the boundaries of simulation, data science, and scientific computing.

Looking forward to creating together,
Carlos Romero Madrid
